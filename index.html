Assignment 01 Multiple linear regression

Predicting Evapotranspiration over Islamabad

Evapotranspiration modelled from satellite-derived vegetation indices and climate variables for the Islamabad Capital Territory: 87 observations, a missing-data and outlier audit, VIF pruning, then a regression checked on a held-out test set and with 5-fold cross-validation.

Test R² 0.785 (RMSE 1.78 mm); cross-validated mean R² 0.724. Six predictors kept after pruning EVI and LAI.

  • scikit-learn
  • statsmodels
  • VIF

Assignment 02 Clustering

Clustering E-commerce Product Offers

Four clustering algorithms on 35,311 product offers: K-means, Ward, DBSCAN and Gaussian mixtures on TF-IDF and LSA features. Compared on 25,311 held-out offers, for stability and for cost, with four written answers and a deployment recommendation.

K-means recommended: ARI 0.62 and NMI 0.70 on the held-out offers, stability 0.82.
